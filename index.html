Our Work

Real Answers. No Runaround.

27 years in, the questions are pretty predictable. Here’s what Brendan hears most — and the straight answers.

How much does junk removal cost on Long Island? +
Pricing depends on the volume and type of items. Most residential loads run from around $150 for a single item to $400–$700 for a full truck. Brendan always gives you a free, no-obligation on-site quote before any work begins — what he quotes is what you pay. No games. Call (631) 848-6762 to get started.
Do you offer same-day junk removal? +
Yes — same-day service is available in most areas. Brendan has been known to arrive within minutes of a call. We’re open 7 days a week, 7:00 AM – 6:00 PM. Call (631) 848-6762 and we’ll do everything we can to get out to you today.
What areas of Long Island do you serve? +
We cover Suffolk County on Long Island’s South Shore and Nassau County. Towns we regularly serve in Suffolk include Sayville, Bayport, Bay Shore, Islip, East Islip, West Islip, Oakdale, Bohemia, Patchogue, Holbrook, Brookhaven, Ronkonkoma, Hauppauge, and Smithtown — plus East Meadow and surrounding Nassau County towns. Not sure if we reach you? Call (631) 848-6762 — we’ll tell you on the spot.
What types of items do you haul away? +
We remove furniture, sofas, mattresses, appliances, refrigerators, washers and dryers, hot tubs, sheds, construction debris, garage and basement clutter, estate contents, storage unit contents, and much more. We do not haul hazardous materials, chemicals, or biohazardous waste.
Do you donate or recycle before disposing? +
Always. Brendan’s process is: donate first (Habitat for Humanity), recycle second, dispose as a last resort. Multiple customers have noted him going out of his way to find a donation home for items rather than sending them to the landfill — that’s not a marketing claim, it’s in the reviews.
Can you handle estate cleanouts and full property clearouts? +
Estate cleanouts are one of Brendan’s specialties. He handles the entire property — attics, basements, every room — with care and without judgment. He is trusted with house keys by out-of-state clients and known for setting aside heirlooms that might otherwise be overlooked. This is exactly the kind of job he takes on.
